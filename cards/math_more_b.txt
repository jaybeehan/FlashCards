## Mathematics 30-1 | Transformations of Functions

Describe y = f(x) - 2. :: Shift f down 2 units.
Describe y = 3f(x). :: Vertical stretch by a factor of 3.
Describe y = f(2x). :: Horizontal compression by a factor of 1/2.
Describe y = f(x/2). :: Horizontal stretch by a factor of 2.
Describe y = -f(x). :: Reflection in the x-axis.

Where does (6, 3) go under y = f(2x)? :: (3, 3)

What is an invariant point? :: A point that does not change under a transformation.
When is a function its own inverse? :: When it is symmetric about y = x, like f(x) = 1/x.

What is the effect of a < 0? :: A vertical reflection in the x-axis (plus stretch of |a|).

What is the order of transformations? :: Stretches and reflections first, then translations.
