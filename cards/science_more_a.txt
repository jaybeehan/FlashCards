## Physics 20 | Circular Motion, Work and Energy
What is the direction of centripetal force? :: Toward the centre of the circle.

What is frequency in circular motion? :: Number of rotations per second (Hz).

## Physics 20 | Oscillatory Motion and Mechanical Waves
What is the amplitude of a wave? :: The maximum displacement from rest position.
What is the wavelength? :: The distance between two successive crests.
What is the frequency of a wave? :: The number of cycles per second (Hz).
Find the frequency: period = 0.5 s. :: 2 Hz
Find the wave speed: f = 10 Hz, wavelength = 3 m. :: 30 m/s
What is a crest? :: The highest point of a transverse wave.
What is a trough? :: The lowest point of a transverse wave.
What is a compression? :: A region of high pressure in a longitudinal wave.
What is a rarefaction? :: A region of low pressure in a longitudinal wave.
What is constructive interference? :: Waves combine to produce a larger amplitude.
What is destructive interference? :: Waves cancel to produce a smaller amplitude.
What is a standing wave? :: A wave pattern that appears stationary, with nodes and antinodes.
What is a node? :: A point of no displacement in a standing wave.
What is an antinode? :: A point of maximum displacement in a standing wave.
What is reflection of a wave? :: A wave bouncing off a boundary.
What is refraction? :: A change in wave direction because of a change in speed.
What is diffraction? :: Wave spreading around obstacles.
What is the pitch of sound related to? :: Frequency.
What is the loudness of sound related to? :: Amplitude.
What is the speed of sound in water compared with air? :: Faster (about 1500 m/s in water).
